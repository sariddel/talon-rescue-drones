Why it's an optimization problem

The same math the Coast Guard uses to plan a search

Coverage decides everything

A search's success rides on coverage — how much swept sensor area you put over the drift circle. C = swept area ÷ search area Each drone adds sweep equal to its sensor width × speed × time on station.

Detection is exponential

Probability of detection follows the random-search law POD = 1 − e−C Doubling coverage doesn't double your odds — the first sweeps matter most, and there are diminishing returns to piling on.

Every drone trades off

Cheap scouts swarm but can't reach a far datum. Long-range thermal reaches anything but eats the budget. Night, sea state, and a quiet submarine all change which sensor wins. There's a best buy — find it.

How to play

You're the search commander. Someone is in the water — or a submarine is closing on your ship. You have a fixed budget and a ticking clock. Spend wisely.

1

Pick a mission. Lost Sailor (SAR) — find a survivor before the survival window closes. Submarine (ASW) — hold a track on a contact before it slips away.

2

Read the briefing. How far is the datum? Is it night? Rough seas? Is the sub running silent or snorkeling? Each condition changes which drone is the right buy.

3

Field your swarm with the + / − steppers. There's no spending limit — but the $ target is your efficiency goal: solve it lean and you score higher. Watch the live detection probability climb as you add drones. A drone greyed-out with a ✕ can't reach the datum: wasted money.

4

Deploy and watch the search play out. You either find the target or run out of time. Then submit your score to the leaderboard.

The optimization — what wins

  • Coverage is everything. Detection rises with how much sensor area you sweep over the search circle.
  • Set your reach. The Drone reach slider sets how far the drones push out — aim it at the datum distance in the briefing. Too short misses entirely; too far thins your coverage.
  • Manual steering (toggle on): after deploy, point or drag anywhere on the ocean and your drones hunt where you aim — find the target yourself before the clock runs out.
  • Far datum? Cheap short-legged SCOUTs burn their fuel just getting there — buy range (RANGER / SENTINEL) or add a forward shore launch.
  • Night? Thermal beats daylight cameras — lean on RANGER / SENTINEL.
  • Silent submarine? Sonobuoys go deaf — only SENTINEL's MAD boom finds it. Snorkeling? It's exposed to radar — cheap swarms work.
  • Spend lean. Coming in under the $ target and finding fast both boost your score; overspending drags it down. The best plan is rarely the most expensive one — that's the whole game.
